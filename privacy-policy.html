Privacy Policy for ScanCopy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026
Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Welcome to ScanCopy ("we", "our", "us", or the "App").

ScanCopy is a document scanning and document management application that allows users to scan documents, extract text using Optical Character Recognition (OCR), import images, create PDF files, edit extracted text, and share documents.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information ScanCopy may access, how that information is used, how it may be stored, and the choices available to you when using the App.

By downloading or using ScanCopy, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Access

1.1 Camera

ScanCopy may request access to your device's camera to allow you to:

  • Scan documents
  • Capture document images
  • Scan identification documents
  • Create digital copies of physical documents

Camera access is used only when you choose to use a scanning or camera-related feature.

ScanCopy does not continuously access or monitor your camera in the background.

1.2 Photos and Images

If you choose to import a document or image from your device, ScanCopy may access the image or file that you select.

Imported images may be used for:

  • Document scanning
  • Image enhancement
  • OCR text extraction
  • PDF creation
  • Document editing

ScanCopy does not need access to unrelated photos or files that you do not select.

1.3 Documents and Files

The App may create, read, modify, or store files necessary to provide document-management functionality.

These may include:

  • Scanned documents
  • Images
  • PDF files
  • Extracted text
  • Application-generated document data

2. Documents and Personal Information

Documents scanned or imported into ScanCopy may contain personal or sensitive information, including names, addresses, identification numbers, signatures, financial information, educational records, or other private information.

Important: You are responsible for the documents you choose to scan, import, store, or share through the App.

ScanCopy does not require you to provide the contents of your documents to us merely to use the basic scanning functionality.

You should avoid scanning or storing information that you do not want stored on your device or shared through your device's sharing functionality.

3. OCR (Optical Character Recognition)

ScanCopy provides OCR functionality that extracts text from images and scanned documents.

OCR processing may include:

  • Detecting text in document images
  • Converting images into editable text
  • Allowing you to review or edit extracted text
  • Using extracted text when creating or managing documents

Where OCR processing is performed on your device, document images and extracted text are processed locally and are not automatically uploaded to our servers solely for OCR processing.

If a particular version or feature of ScanCopy uses an external processing service, information may be transmitted to that service as necessary to provide that feature.

4. How We Use Information

Information accessed by ScanCopy is used to provide and improve the App's functionality, including:

  • Scanning documents
  • Detecting document boundaries
  • Enhancing scanned images
  • Performing OCR
  • Creating PDF documents
  • Saving documents
  • Editing extracted text
  • Importing documents
  • Organizing documents
  • Sharing documents when requested by the user
  • Maintaining and improving application performance
  • Detecting and resolving technical problems

We do not use the contents of your scanned documents for advertising purposes.

5. Local Storage

ScanCopy may store documents and related information locally on your device.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Scanned document images
  • Imported images
  • PDF files
  • OCR text
  • Document names
  • Folder information
  • Application settings

Information stored locally remains on your device unless you explicitly share, export, back up, or otherwise transfer it using your device or another service.

Deleting the App may remove locally stored application data depending on your Android device and backup settings.

6. Sharing and Exporting Documents

ScanCopy may provide functionality that allows you to share or export documents.

You may choose to:

  • Share a PDF
  • Share an image
  • Share extracted text
  • Export a document
  • Open a document using another application

When you use these functions, Android or another application may transfer the selected information to the destination you choose.

ScanCopy does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications or services that you choose to use for sharing or exporting documents.

Please review the privacy policies of those services before sharing sensitive information.

7. Internet Access

Certain versions or features of ScanCopy may require Internet access.

Internet access may be used for:

  • Application functionality
  • Application updates
  • Error reporting
  • Third-party services
  • Optional online features

If a feature does not require Internet access, ScanCopy does not intentionally transmit your document contents simply because you are using the application.

8. Third-Party Services

ScanCopy may use third-party libraries or services to provide certain functionality, improve application reliability, or support technical operations.

These services may include Android system services, machine-learning libraries, OCR libraries, crash-reporting services, analytics services, or other technical services where required by the App.

Third-party services may process limited technical information or information necessary to provide their specific functionality.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party services integrated into the App.

9. Advertising

ScanCopy does not intentionally use the contents of your scanned documents, OCR text, or personal documents to create advertising profiles.

If advertising is introduced in a future version of the App, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the relevant advertising practices.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device, including using a secure screen lock, password, PIN, or biometric authentication where appropriate.

11. Children's Privacy

ScanCopy is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for the purpose of creating user profiles or accounts.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

12. Your Choices

You can control many permissions through your Android device settings.

You may:

  • Deny camera permission
  • Deny or restrict photo/file access
  • Remove previously granted permissions
  • Delete documents stored by the App
  • Uninstall the App

Restricting certain permissions may prevent specific ScanCopy features from working properly.

13. Data Retention

Documents and other information stored locally by ScanCopy generally remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App, subject to your Android device's storage and backup behavior.

We do not intentionally retain copies of documents on our servers unless a feature specifically requires such processing or storage.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, technology, legal requirements, or our practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
